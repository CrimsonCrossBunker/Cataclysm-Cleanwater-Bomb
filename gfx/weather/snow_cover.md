# Snow cover texture

The 512 × 384 sheet contains 32 × 32 terrain overlays. The bands are light,
medium, and deep snow. Each band has four rows of rotated variations and
sixteen columns of edge profiles. Bits 0–3 of the column are north, east,
south, and west. Equal profile edges use matching symmetric pixels.

The sheet mixes the original snow with a new ImageGen grayscale density
map of irregular snow patches, crust, and powder without a dominant
direction. New CC0 references were [Snow 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_01),
[Snow 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_03), and [Snow Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_floor).
Original references were [Snow 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_02),
[Asphalt Snow](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_snow), and
[Snow Field Aerial](https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_field_aerial).
