# North American vegetation and settlement

The atlas uses the Bedford projection documented in [north_america.md](north_america.md).
The ecology grid samples the continent at 8.64 km intervals. Its 49 CEC level II
regions are mapped to 22 gameplay vegetation communities. Low-resolution forest
coverage biases the existing continuous forest noise. Population density changes the
frequency of procedural towns, and the original Natural Earth points still seed named
cities with population-based sizes. Roads, buildings, and individual stands are
generated by the game. These layers apply when generating previously unexplored
terrain in North American atlas worlds.

The editable community weights and plant IDs are in
`north_america_ecology_profiles.json`. A nonempty `range` specifies a mapped native
range. An empty field means the plant's occurrence is based on the listed ecology
communities, which are broader than precise native-range polygons. The Sonoran
community is constrained to an approximate saguaro-range polygon. All new terrain
IDs have existing ASCII and tileset fallbacks.

## Data credits

- **Ecoregions.** Commission for Environmental Cooperation (CEC), 2021,
  [North American Environmental Atlas, Ecological Regions, Level II](https://www.cec.org/north-american-environmental-atlas/terrestrial-ecoregions-level-ii/),
  with Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada, the U.S. EPA, INEGI, CONABIO, and INECC.
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Generalized and rasterized for the game.
- **Forest cover.** CEC, NRCan/CCRS, USGS, INEGI, CONABIO, and CONAFOR,
  [NALCMS North American Land Cover, 2010 (MODIS)](https://www.cec.org/north-american-environmental-atlas/land-cover-2010-modis-250m/).
  The game samples the USFS service and aggregates classes 1–6 to approximate
  forest fractions. See the [CEC data reuse terms](https://www.cec.org/sites/default/atlas/).
- **Population.** Schiavina, M., Freire, S., Carioli, A., and MacManus, K. (2023),
  [GHS-POP R2023A](https://doi.org/10.2905/2FF68A52-5B5B-4A22-8F40-C41DA8332CFE),
  European Commission Joint Research Centre, 2020 epoch.
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  The 16 km overview of the 1 km Mollweide data is reprojected and reduced to
  eight density bands.
- **Native ranges.** USDA Forest Service, Elbert L. Little Jr.,
  [Atlas of United States Trees](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/treedistributionmaps),
  digitized by USGS and preserved in the [USTreeAtlas mirror](https://github.com/wpetry/USTreeAtlas).
  These public domain vectors are rasterized to determine local plant eligibility.
  Additional coarse habitat assignments use [USDA Silvics of North America](https://research.fs.usda.gov/products/dataandtools/silvics-north-america)
  and CONABIO profiles for [netleaf oak](https://www.conabio.gob.mx/conocimiento/info_especies/arboles/doctos/31-fagac10m.pdf)
  and [gumbo-limbo](https://www.conabio.gob.mx/conocimiento/info_especies/arboles/doctos/17-burse2m.pdf).

## Regeneration

Use Python 3.12 with NumPy 2.5.4, PyProj 3.8.0, Rasterio 1.5.0 and Shapely 2.2.0.
Place the following inputs in one directory.

1. Save `eco2-main.geojson` from the [Level II FeatureServer query](https://services7.arcgis.com/oF9CDB4lUYF7Um9q/ArcGIS/rest/services/NA_Terrestrial_Ecoregions_Level_2/FeatureServer/3/query),
   with `where=Area_SqKm>100`, `outFields=LEVEL1,LEVEL2,NameL2_En,Area_SqKm`,
   `outSR=4326`, `geometryPrecision=3`, `maxAllowableOffset=0.04` and `f=geojson`.
2. Copy the GeoJSON for each nonempty `range` from [the mirror's geojson directory](https://github.com/wpetry/USTreeAtlas/tree/main/geojson).
3. Read the [2020 population COG](https://jrc-ghsl.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/ghs-pop/r2023a/54009/1000m/2020/GHS_POP_E2020_GLOBE_R2023A_54009_1000_V1_0.tif)
   with Rasterio `OVERVIEW_LEVEL=3`. Save `population.npz` via
   `np.savez_compressed(path, data=dataset.read(1), affine=tuple(dataset.transform), nodata=dataset.nodata)`.
4. Export arrays from the [USFS ImageServer](https://imagery.geoplatform.gov/iipp/rest/services/Vegetation/USFS_EDW_CEC_LandCoverOfNorthAmerica_2010_250m/ImageServer/exportImage)
   with `bboxSR=4326`, `imageSR=4326`, `format=tiff`, `pixelType=U8`,
   `interpolation=RSP_NearestNeighbor`, `adjustAspectRatio=false`,
   `renderingRule={"rasterFunction":"None"}` and `f=image`. Use these bounding
   boxes and sizes, with longitude first.

   | Filename | Bounds | Size |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | `landcover-west.tif` | `-180,11,-112,84` | `830,900` |
   | `landcover-east.tif` | `-112,11,-48,84` | `790,900` |
   | `landcover-aleutian.tif` | `167,48,180,57` | `260,180` |

Run:

```sh
python tools/generate_north_america_ecology.py /path/to/sources data/raw/north_america_ecology_profiles.json data/raw/north_america.ecology
```

Pass `--check` to verify the checked-in output.
